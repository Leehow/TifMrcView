# Code of conduct

TifMrcView is built for scientists and anyone who works with microscope
images. Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests,
discussions or email, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, and assume good intent;
- give and take criticism of code and ideas, never of people;
- keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, insults and
  discrimination of any kind;
- respect other people's data and privacy: do not post images or files you
  are not allowed to share.

Behaviour that breaks these rules can be reported to the maintainer, Hao Li
(lihao9x@iccas.ac.cn). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer
may remove comments, close issues or block participants who do not follow
this code of conduct.
